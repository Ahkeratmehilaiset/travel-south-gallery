Aramu Muru (Hayu Marca)

A perfect doorway, about seven metres square, cut into a red cliff, with no room behind it.

Near Lake Titicaca, Hayu Marca, the 'gate of the gods', is a smooth niche with a small T-shaped recess at its base like a keyhole. A popular story tells of a priest named Aramu Muru who passed through it carrying a golden disk. It became widely known in the early 1990s.

But... who carved it, and when? No archaeological study has dated it; researchers consider an unfinished Inca shrine one plausible explanation, while some enthusiasts ask whether it is a 'stargate', a claim with no archaeological support.
