Salar de Uyuni

This salt flat is so flat that it is used to calibrate satellite altimeters.

Salar de Uyuni is the largest salt flat on Earth, over 10,000 square km of white left by vanished prehistoric lakes. Cactus-covered Isla Incahuasi breaks the horizon, salt workers at Colchani cut blocks by hand, and outside Uyuni rusting steam locomotives recall the mining railways.

But... beneath the crust lies one of the world's largest lithium deposits. Researchers estimate the reserves are huge; some ask what extracting them will do to this landscape.

Would you rather see it dry and white, or as a mirror after rain?
