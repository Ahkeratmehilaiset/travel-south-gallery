Chucuito: Inca Uyo

Dozens of stone phalluses stand upright inside a small walled enclosure by Lake Titicaca.

Inca Uyo in the town of Chucuito is nicknamed the 'fertility temple'. Chucuito was the capital of the Lupaqa, an Aymara kingdom on the lake before the Inca conquest, and the finely fitted outer wall may well be Inca work.

But... how old are the carved stones? Several researchers argue many show little sign of age and were arranged or added in the 20th century for visitors; guides and some enthusiasts ask whether it was an ancient sacred site after all.

Ancient temple or tourist invention: what is your verdict?
