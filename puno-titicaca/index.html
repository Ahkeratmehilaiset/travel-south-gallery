Puno & Lake Titicaca

Andean myth says the sun itself was born from this lake.

Puno sits at about 3,800 m on Lake Titicaca, often called the highest navigable lake in the world. From the harbour, boats run to the Uros, whose islands are woven from totora reeds and must be constantly rebuilt. The lake is also named in myth as the birthplace of the first Inca, Manco Capac.

But... how long have the Uros lived on floating islands? Researchers estimate the tradition is old but has changed greatly with tourism; some enthusiasts ask how much of the original way of life survives.

Could you live on an island you have to rebuild every year?
