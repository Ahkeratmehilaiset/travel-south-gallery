Travel South: photos and stories

In 2011 I photographed the stone structures of Peru and Bolivia. In 2014 my camera recorded skulls in Peruvian museums, and in one photo I hold a skull that the collection calls Starchild 2. In February 2024 I photographed Amarna-period stone heads in Cairo. These pictures give this site its question: what did my camera record, what can research tell us about it, and what kind of world do the shapes bring to mind?

Starchild 2 is a name given by the collection. It is borrowed from another skull, known from Mexico, which Lloyd Pye brought to public attention. This gallery keeps the two skulls apart.

What research has found

  • Nazca Lines A study published in 2024 found 303 new figures in addition to the 430 known before. (Sakai et al. 2024)
  • Machu Picchu According to radiocarbon dates, the site was in use by about 1420, earlier than inferred from the chronicles. (Burger et al. 2021)
  • Puma Punku Construction began about AD 580 and ended about AD 710, centuries before the Inca. (Marsh et al. 2023)
  • Paracas Of 159 people studied from the Cerro Colorado tombs, 98 per cent had a skull shaped in childhood. (Gómez-Mejía et al. 2022)
  • Cairo and Peru The long heads of Amarna are sculptures; the skulls of Peru are bone. The similarity of the shapes is visual, not evidence of a connection.
