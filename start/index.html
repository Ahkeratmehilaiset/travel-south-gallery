Travel South: photographs and stories

Travel South is Jani Korpi’s journeys with a camera. In 2011 he photographed Peru and Bolivia in travel order, from the Nazca coast to the salt of Uyuni: desert, reed islands, the walls of Sacsayhuamán above Cusco, and the stones of Tiwanaku and Puma Punku.

In 2014, in Peru, he photographed skulls and filmed museum footage on 1 November 2014. One photograph from that visit shows him holding the skull that the collection calls Starchild 2. The name is borrowed from a different skull in Mexico, made known by Lloyd Pye; the gallery keeps the two apart. In studied Paracas material, a long head is read as childhood modification, not as a result of DNA.

On 15 February 2024 he photographed stone heads in the Egyptian Museum in Cairo. Their profile can recall the elongated skulls of Peru, but the likeness is visual only: the Cairo heads are sculptures, the Peruvian skulls are bone.

“I have travelled all over the globe, and here I am holding the Starchild 2 skull in my hands.”

Jani Korpi on photo 04, message of 20 November 2026 (wording smoothed, translated from Finnish)
