Machu Picchu

The Spanish never found Machu Picchu, yet local farmers were living there when Hiram Bingham arrived in 1911.

High on a ridge above the Urubamba, Machu Picchu was built as a royal estate, most likely for the Inca ruler Pachacuti. Radiocarbon dating published in 2021 shows people were living here by about AD 1420. Its granite walls lock together without mortar, and near the June solstice a play of light can be seen in the curved Torreón (Ziółkowski & Kościuk 2020): a light effect around the solstice, not a precise calendar.

But... researchers estimate the finest stonework is Inca; some enthusiasts, such as Brien Foerster, ask whether some of it belongs to an older culture, a view mainstream archaeology rejects.
