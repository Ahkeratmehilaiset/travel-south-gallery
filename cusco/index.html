Cusco

When earthquakes hit Cusco in 1650 and 1950, colonial buildings cracked or fell while the Inca walls beneath them held.

Cusco was the heart of the Inca Empire. Spanish churches and mansions sit directly on Inca walls, like the palace wall on Hatun Rumiyoc street with its famous twelve-angled stone. Archaeologists date the finest masonry to the 15th century.

But... researchers estimate the megalithic foundations and the cruder upper courses reflect different Inca building phases and repairs; some enthusiasts, such as Brien Foerster, ask whether they are the work of an older builder culture.

Which do you notice first in Cusco, the Inca walls or the churches on top of them?
