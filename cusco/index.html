Cusco

← Aramu Muru (Hayu Marca)All destinationsSacsayhuamán →

The Inca walls of Cusco show the marks of two strong earthquakes. One of them struck in Inca times (Combey et al. 2022).

Cusco was the capital of the Inca Empire. UNESCO describes the city as an amalgam of the Inca capital and the colonial city (UNESCO). The twelve-angled stone on Hatun Rumiyoc street was part of the wall of Inca Roca's palace (Smithsonian 3D). Paint was sprayed on the stone in March 2014, and on 18 February 2025 it was struck with a metal object (Ministry of Culture 2025).

Speculation: Brien Foerster asks whether the megalithic foundations are the work of an older builder culture. Protzen and Nair compared Inca and Tiwanaku joints and did not find the same technique (Protzen and Nair 1997).
